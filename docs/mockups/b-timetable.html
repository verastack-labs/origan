Four strands, running the whole time.

Not twelve features to read through. Four things that are always happening, doing different work depending on the year a student is in.

Direction

Knowing what to learn next

A questionnaire produces a roadmap. Curated playlists sit inside each block, and a glossary turns the jargon into something a first-year can actually hold.

Strongest in the first two years, when the cost of drifting is highest and nobody notices it happening.

Practice

Doing the work, repeatedly

Curated DSA sheets with revisit marking, and a test engine covering aptitude, reasoning, and technical topics rather than saving all of it for final year.

Compounds. The reason a student arrives at campus training already able to use it.

Readiness

Turning preparation into applications

Resume tooling, template galleries, and Avsar, which moves campus drives off WhatsApp groups and Google Forms into one place students actually apply from.

Third and fourth year. Also where the outcome data comes from.

Evidence

What the college can see

Trajectory across years rather than a readiness score on a student. Reporting arrives biweekly or monthly, shaped with your placement cell.

Designed so students never experience it as being watched.
