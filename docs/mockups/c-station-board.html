Scheduled haltsOne cohort, entry to placement
YR I

Direction

A questionnaire produces a roadmap. Curated playlists sit inside each block, and a glossary makes the jargon usable.

The college sees a first-year cohort engaging rather than drifting, at the point where drifting is invisible and expensive.
YR II

Practice

Curated DSA sheets with revisit marking. Aptitude, reasoning, and technical tests, spread across years rather than crammed into the last one.

The college sees which strands a batch is weak in, early enough to actually do something about it.
YR III

Readiness

Resume tooling and template galleries. Avsar moves campus drives and internship applications off WhatsApp groups and Google Forms.

The college sees internship conversion, which is the first honest leading indicator of placement.
YR IV

Season

Drives, applications, interviews. Standing on three years of accumulated work rather than a fortnight of it.

The college sees participation and outcomes in one place instead of a spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group.
