Four-year engagement
1.0

Preparation begins in the first year, not placement week.

A training programme arrives in the final year, runs for a fortnight against a fixed syllabus, and leaves. By then the students who were going to be ready already were.

Origan is in place from the first year and stays for four. A consultant assigned to your college talks to your students, learns what they are actually stuck on, and that understanding is built back into the platform your students use every week.

3.0

Alongside your training, not instead of it.

Keep your aptitude trainers and your company-specific programmes. Origan is not competing for that line item. It changes what students are like when they walk into those sessions.

A training programme

  • Final year, when the season has already started
  • A fortnight, then it ends
  • The same syllabus at every college
  • Nothing measured after the trainer leaves

Origan

  • First year onward, all four
  • Continuous, and extended as it runs
  • Shaped by what your students report
  • Instrumented from the first week to the last
