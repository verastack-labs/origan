1.0

Preparation begins in the first year, not placement week.

A training programme arrives in the final year, runs a fixed syllabus for a fortnight, and leaves. By then the students who were going to be ready already were, and the ones who were not have run out of time.

Origan is in place from the first semester and stays for four years. A consultant assigned to your college talks to your students, learns what they are actually stuck on, and that understanding is built back into the platform they use every week.

2.0

What the four years actually contain.

Not a course to finish, and not twelve features to read through. Four strands that are always running, doing different work depending on the year a student is in.

Engagement by year of study
YearThe studentYour placement cell

3.0

Alongside your training, not instead of it.

Keep your aptitude trainers and your company-specific programmes. Origan is not competing for that line item. It changes what your students are like when they walk into those sessions.

A training programme

  • Final year, with the season already running
  • A fortnight, and then it ends
  • The same syllabus at every college
  • Nothing measured once the trainer leaves

Origan, alongside it

  • First year onward, all four
  • Continuous, and extended as it runs
  • Shaped by what your students report
  • Instrumented from the first week to the last
4.0

A consultant who has actually sat with your students.

Assigned to your college for the length of the partnership. They sit with students, five to ten a day on campus days, and find out what is genuinely in the way rather than what a syllabus assumes. What they learn becomes the roadmap. That is the part a platform cannot do alone, and the reason Origan does not become another login your students ignore.
